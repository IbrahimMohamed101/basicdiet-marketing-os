# Basic Diet — Google Flow / AI video production recipe

This is a project-specific companion to .agents/skills/video/SKILL.md and, for user-requested detailed storyboards, .agents/skills/storyboard-to-video/SKILL.md. Consult those skills **first**. The procedure produces prompts and preproduction unless the user explicitly requests actual video generation.

## Select a production mode

1. **Preferred for authentic food:** edit real Basic Diet footage; maintain food identity, packaging and consistent grading.
2. **Image-to-video:** start from a visually inspected, licensed, exact Drive photograph. Animate camera/light/environment gently. Do not transform the food into a different dish.
3. **Text-to-video:** only for generic conceptual B-roll without claims of documenting real food, team, branch or customer. Label conceptual.
4. **Complex recurring scenes:** when expressly requested, use storyboard-to-video for beat list, reference boards, anchor continuity and final prompt. Do not auto-create boards merely from reading a skill.

## Preflight checklist

- Task brief: funnel goal, specific audience and pain/benefit, CTA, platform, delivery format, length and safe margins.
- Asset anchor: real Drive ID, actual visual inspection, dimensions/crop, rights confirmation and current menu truth; list missing items honestly.
- Brand anchor: visually inspected approved logo, pack shots and actual palette; if unknown, omit exact colors. Overlays/legible Arabic are made in an editor, not trusted to text generation.
- Delivery: Instagram Reels / TikTok = vertical 9:16; feed posts may need a separate crop; landing-page desktop hero may be 16:9 with mobile-safe focal area. Never stretch source footage.
- Paid media is blocked until owner approval and economics/attribution review.

## Shot design

For each shot record: beat and duration, exact source image/video ID, camera direction, product consistency constraints, action, lighting/mood, on-screen copy added later, audio treatment, continuity and potential model failure. Use one intelligible reveal per shot; inspect hand/food morphing, package logos, Arabic text and crop.

## Output to retain for an approved creative

One production brief and chosen image/video source IDs; 3–5 beat timeline; paste-ready model prompt; negative prompt; exact Arabic overlays/caption typed separately; alternate fallback using real footage; review checklist and status. Save under content/production/ only when the concept is actually selected. Record model/tool and observed outcome after execution, not before.

No AI video runtime is automatically connected by this repository; Google Flow usage and cost/credit requirements must be checked in its own interface.
