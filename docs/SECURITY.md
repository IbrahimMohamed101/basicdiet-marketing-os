# Privacy, spend and publishing safeguards

- Repository visibility must be **private**. Never assume 'private' is an excuse to commit personal data.
- No real customer full names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, payment details or raw support messages in git.
- Never commit credentials, .env, Firebase service accounts, API tokens, campaign access tokens or signed URLs.
- Do not copy entire MongoDB collections or platform exports into the repo.
- Aggregate business snapshots should record definition, reporting timezone (Saudi Arabia / Asia-Riyadh for commercial reporting), period, source, captured-at and analyst caveats.
- Paid ads, public publishing, offers/price changes and external messages require explicit human approval before action.
- Store only asset manifests / canonical URLs; no full raw videos, photo originals, paid licensed assets or unreviewed third-party skills.
- Keep upstream licenses and attribution with any imported external skill.
- Never claim AI controls a platform or has access without an actual integration and permission.
