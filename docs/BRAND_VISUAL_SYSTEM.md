**Never** crop away Arabic from a logo, invent a new leaf/dumbbell emblem, change green to the competitor's lime color, retype the logo or use loud full-surface gradients everywhere. No logo is considered "officially approved" merely because we have the PNG.

## Scale and safe space

- Feed native 1080×1350: content-safe inset 72px (minimum design suggestion), headline should dominate and be legible at thumbnail/mobile size. Keep title around 1–2 short lines and body copy optional.
- Story native 1080×1920: reserve generous top/bottom UI-safe zones; keep main subject/CTA in middle, not beneath the platform username/reply bar.
- Carousel 1080×1350 per slide: identical logo position, outer margin and lower progress treatment. First slide's hook must stand alone.
- Square 1080×1080 is useful for specific placements, not the default when a 4:5 feed visual is intended.
- Size / contrast / overlay must be verified visually on actual final export. These pixel numbers are working proposals, not platform-certified safe zones.

## Repeatable layouts, varied narrative

1. **Hero/product:** 65–75% prominent *unaltered* photo in the central/lower area; one Saudi hook top; micro footer.
2. **Choice:** 2–3 real food images with big 1/2/3 and a single clear question, same rounded-corner/card style.
3. **Editorial normal post:** strong hook in center on cream abstract canvas, original logo top, one simple familiar illustration; no unnecessary food or sales offer.
4. **Offer:** one verified numerical headline on clean branded field; show restrictions only when verified; no fictional price/sale.
5. **Story micro:** one photo or pure typography, simple question/poll with real platform interactive tools added later.

A successful grid has a **family resemblance without clones**: share logo, greens/oranges, cream field, heading hierarchy and footer line, while alternating hero, choice, editorial and Story designs. Avoid an endless feed of the same 3-card composition.

## Consistency check (human-visible)

Before each export ask: Can you identify Basic Diet with the text hidden? Is the original food still the same actual dish? Is the hook readable on a 6-inch display? Does Arabic shape correctly? Are all claims and menu details grounded? Does this layout look like part of the previous 3 posts without appearing duplicated? If any answer is no, revise.
