## Editorial sorting instructions for agents

1. **Archive/input:** RAW ARW and original camera JPG; preserve source and do not silently move, rename or compress.
2. **Production candidates:** edited JPEG folders; index exact IDs, check source-vs-edit content before declaring a unique shot, inspect actual pixels, then assign a use-case.
3. **A / use first as draft candidate:** food prep/assembly branded closeups, presentation to camera, fruit/salad hero images. Human images remain rights-blocked.
4. **B / secondary:** alternate angles, ingredient details, plated mains pending verified menu identity.
5. **Not media:** Windows shortcut (`.lnk`). Ignore in creative selection.
6. **Never infer motion from these stills.** A slideshow/photo-animation is not newly shot video. Use verified older footage if a genuine real-motion reel is needed.
7. **No auto-publish:** check current menu availability, talent rights, restaurant image rights, authentic logo, typography, linked destination, and complete vertical composition.
