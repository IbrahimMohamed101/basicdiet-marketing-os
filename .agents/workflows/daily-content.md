# Daily content workflow — manual contract

Trigger: `Basic Diet Mode — ننزل إيه النهارده؟`

1. Read `STATE.md`, native skill, content log/backlog (once migrated), verified current offer and authorized asset catalog.
2. Check whether a similar post was published recently; do not repeat without reason.
3. Produce one approval-ready idea with: goal, funnel stage, hook, shot list/script, caption, CTA, stories, approved media reference, KPI, paid-potential.
4. Ask for approval before actually publishing or spending anything.
5. Record published results only after publication is verified.

**Phase 0:** specification only. No scheduler, social account integration, or automatic uploads.
