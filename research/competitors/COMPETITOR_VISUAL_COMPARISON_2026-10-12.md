**Basis:** 2026-10-12 user-provided Instagram profile-grid screenshot collages: 3 for @diet_hubsa, 4 for @healthy_corner_sa. Mechanism-level comparison only, **not effectiveness, posting rate, pricing, revenue or verified Reels review**.

## Observed creative tendencies (not mutually exclusive)

| Feature | Diet Hub screenshot sample | Healthy Corner screenshot sample | Basic Diet opportunity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Repeated brand anchors | Circular top logo, orange/green/dark-green accents | Dark green + bright green/lime fields, recurring corners/logo/QR-like footer | Original Basic Diet candidate circular logo, sampled green/orange and cream visual rail |
| Highest-visibility attention cue | Big food trays, dramatic composites, prop/metaphor, gym/location/clock | Food close-ups, colloquial statement/question, person-to-camera-style thumbnails and occasional meme | Alternate product/prop-led hero with short honest message and real evidence |
| Content variation | Fitness/lifestyle, packaging, national/seasonal campaigns, app, offers | Product, FAQs, informal human voice, humor, app, offers, seasonal, behind scenes | Test **different communication jobs**, not only redesigned sale banners |
| Composition | Often layered/montaged scenes with props and large headlines | Mixed minimalist single-plate, vivid title-only cards and humans/real-looking video covers | Use one hero or one thought per post with tighter mobile legibility |
| Conversion bridge | Discount card, app mockup, bag/delivery | Package hierarchy, actual-looking app screen, FAQ/on-camera explainer | Show **real Basic Diet app** choice path with verified facts and clear CTA |
| Weakness/risk | Potential crowding and exaggerated layered props | Lime/brand saturation, tiny footer, small offer/legal text | Keep more whitespace and authentic photographed food; no copied palettes or claims |

## Design system decision for Basic Diet

**Keep own identity, introduce editorial variety.** Maintain only **own verified/provisional** design ingredients from `assets/visual-identity.json` and `docs/BRAND_VISUAL_SYSTEM.md`: original logo pixels (pending owner selection), green `#107F55`, orange `#E95D2C` (sampled provisional), airy cream `#F7F4E6` (design proposal), original RTL type hierarchy, unaltered photographed food.

**A useful 9-piece portfolio to test** (NOT a fixed calendar or promised performance mix):

| Slot | Content job | Format recommendation | Independent visual mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Appetite | 4:5 true meal hero | Full-height food photograph, very little type |
| B | Trust | Carousel from existing footage frames | Two authentic preparation steps |
| C | Explain | 4:5 carousel | 3 genuine app interface states |
| D | Relatable situation | 4:5 static | Workday context around genuine meal |
| E | Dialogue | Story or 4:5 card | Question-led choice with two current dishes |
| F | Appetite variety | 4:5 static | Extreme close crop from original verified dish |
| G | No-pressure editorial | 4:5 static | Minimal title and one original daily-planning pictogram |
| H | Process / authentic motion | 9:16 real-footage Reel | Source-video sequence, actual food handling |
| I | Clear offer / objection | 4:5 carousel | Only documented active price/plan, or factual subscription FAQ if offers unverified |

**Do not prescribe slot cadence without results.** Before producing a post, check relevant live Basic Diet sources (Drive pixels and rights, current app/menu, logo and CTA). If footage is unavailable, produce an excellent **static post**, not an 8-sec faux-motion poster by default.

## Minimum agent decision rubric

For each of 3 proposed concepts: note competitor **mechanism source ID**, audience hypothesis, single communication job, distinct original visual composition, current authentic asset ID or explicit blocker, post format, short Saudi hook, one CTA, and one same-platform measurable KPI. Reject copied arrangements, neon-lime imitation, new invented footage, false reviews, false menu or price figures, fake app screens, and illegible Arabic. Human approval before publication.

**Research does not establish winners.** Only measure outcomes for verified Basic Diet posts later; do not write competitor screenshot impressions to commercial analytics or `content/winners.md`.
