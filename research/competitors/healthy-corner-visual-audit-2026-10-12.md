## Distinct mechanisms seen (transfer the logic, never copy execution)

### HC-01 — Recognizable brand repetition with variable content
The same visual brand elements recur, but the actual layout alternates between lime headline cards, food hero photos, phone/app frames, interview-like video thumbnails, deep-dark packages and vivid seasonal illustrations.

**Basic Diet:** use its **own** provisional green #107F55 / orange #E95D2C / cream #F7F4E6 system and original candidate logo (not Healthy Corner lime). A family must stay recognizable without repeated cloned compositions. Avoid the same three-meal arrangement all week.

### HC-02 — Immediate appetite: real meal as the star
Many tiles make one realistic-looking tray or plate 50–75% of the thumbnail and reduce supporting copy. Some show hands holding containers; other shots are isolated on contrasting color.

**Basic Diet:** clip/mask a **visually inspected** real food photograph from Drive without synthesizing ingredients, food quantity or logo, and let the subject dominate. Verify it remains on the current menu.

### HC-03 — Human voice/trust instead of generic claims
Multiple tiles present human faces, in-store conversations and staff/customer-looking food demos; screenshot evidence cannot establish testimony truth or whether these are ads.

**Basic Diet:** when existing authorized real video exists, cut real kitchen/plating/packing details. Do not fabricate a talking customer, review, employee role or before/after effect. No additional filming can be assumed available.

### HC-04 — Daily friction/question/punchline
Visible examples include question-style subscription/app posts, motivational or playful statements, colloquial headlines and meal-choice framing. The question is often the dominant thumb-size text.

**Basic Diet:** use original short Saudi Arabic, one audience problem per creative: `الغدا وسط الدوام؟`, `ودّك تشوف كيف تختار وجبتك؟`; avoid reusing any competitor slogan verbatim. Be specific in the caption.

### HC-05 — Conversion-oriented clarity at package and app stage
Package-price cards have striking numeric hierarchy; other tiles present a mobile phone / interface and route the audience to the next action.

**Basic Diet:** show authentic current in-app selectors with privacy-safe screenshots. Only show verified live package/discount/fulfilment totals, never competitor offer details or historical draft figures. Objective is a qualified subscription inquiry or first-time **paid** subscription, not an assumed sale from views.

### HC-06 — Human + information + entertainment in one feed
Humor/memes, suspense, audience questions and cultural context are interleaved with appetite and offers. This is a **varied editorial strategy**, not necessarily a performance-effective mix.

**Basic Diet:** rotate appetite, trust, how-it-works, day-in-life and occasional verified offers. First optimize feasibility from existing source assets before prescribing frequency.

### HC-07 — Seasonal and local resonance
Ramadan/iftar, Saudi-national motifs, football and New Year-like cards appear; some past graphic promotions use expired date or offer imagery.

**Basic Diet:** only use current event dates, local context and individually approved promotions; don't copy seasonal scenes or old events as current.

### HC-08 — One instantly decoded graphic metaphor
A scale, calendar, road sign, gift, bell, app phone or clock can serve as a quick cue. Some samples become visually crowded.

**Basic Diet:** one fresh, original metaphor supported by a realistic food photo and short Saudi hook; keep text clear at mobile grid size.

## What to avoid

- Don't adopt the competitor's neon-lime palette, exact tree logo, staff/face imagery, branded shopping bags or ad-card composition.
- Don't imitate promotional prices, macros, "zero sugar", free-delivery claims, testimonials, exact bundle durations or package inclusions; Basic Diet facts need its current official source.
- Some promotional cards use multiple tiny labels, dense typography or multiple messages; do **not** copy that clutter into a small mobile post.
- Don't mistake highly designed static artworks for convincing video content. The **2026-10-12 Basic Diet TikTok weak-distribution incident** means avoid returning to moving-poster Reels by default.
- Avoid treating pictures of people as documented proof of real customers, permissions or product reviews; all remain unverified.
