# Marketing claim register — draft, 2026-10-09

A claim without a fresh, attributable official source is **BLOCKED from final marketing copy**, not necessarily false. This register tracks review needs, not the complete regulatory rules of Saudi Arabia; consult authoritative current Saudi food advertising/health claims rules before paid campaigns.

| Proposed claim | Status | Necessary evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Restaurant identity: Basic Diet / بيسك دايت | Observed visual logo; brand owner final approval pending | Approved official brand mark |
| Healthy meal subscriptions are available | Historical product context; verify current offering | Current app / official checkout |
| Specific dish e.g. "دجاج بالزبدة" is sold now | UNVERIFIED CURRENT MENU | Current product ID / menu date |
| 100g / 150g / 200g, 1–5 meals/day | HISTORICAL, NEEDS CURRENT CHECK | Current package editor/API |
| KSA96 or BASIC15 discount amount and eligibility | UNVERIFIED CURRENT OFFER | Live validated promo and final checkout calculation |
| Calories, protein, ingredients, allergens | UNVERIFIED | Official current nutritional data per exact portion |
| Delivery cities/fees/schedule | UNVERIFIED | Current fulfillment rules |
| Customer testimonials, ratings, transformations | BLOCKED until real consent/source | Verifiable quote + approval + no medical implication |
| Medical/weight-loss outcomes | BLOCKED pending qualified regulatory/legal evidence | Approved substantiation and appropriate Saudi advertising guidance |
| "No 1", "best", absolute guaranteed results | BLOCKED | Independent substantiation (avoid by default) |

For each verified claim, record `claim_id`, exact authorized source, captured date, validity constraints, verifier and next review date. Missing a claim row does not imply permission to state it.
