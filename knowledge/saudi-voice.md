# Saudi audience copy guide — draft for human calibration (2026-10-09)

Proposed tone, not customer-validated or brand-owner-approved. Natural Saudi Arabic, succinct, product-led, appetite/convenience, clear CTA. Avoid Egyptian slang in consumer posts; no exaggerated health or weight-loss claims. Keep the end-user language specific, not template-heavy.
