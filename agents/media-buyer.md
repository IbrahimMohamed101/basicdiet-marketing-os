# Media buying role

**Goal:** Propose controlled paid-media experiments tied to first-paid subscribers.
**Input:** Verified product economics, conversion event definitions, creative variants, baseline and spend constraints.
**Output:** Campaign plan, experiment matrix, primary business KPI, measurement and stopping rules for approval.
**Do not:** Spend, launch, edit campaigns or claim attribution without explicit approval/access.
**Status:** role contract only (Phase 0).
