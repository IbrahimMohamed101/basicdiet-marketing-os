### EXP-001 — Familiar-food positioning vs generic healthy-language

**Status:** Proposed  
**Created:** 2026-10-07  
**Objective:** Test whether familiar-food creative creates stronger commercial intent than generic healthy-lifestyle messaging.

**Hypothesis:** Creative centered on familiar meals (for example Saudi/familiar dishes) will generate stronger checkout intent than generic "healthy lifestyle" copy because it reduces the perceived taste/sacrifice cost of dieting.

**Evidence behind hypothesis:** Basic Diet has broad familiar-menu assets and nutrition/product structure. This is strategic reasoning, not yet customer-proven VOC.

**Primary metric:** Checkout-to-paid and/or first-paid-subscriber rate when attribution is available.  
**Secondary:** qualified landing/profile visits and checkout starts.  
**Guardrail:** do not use unsupported nutrition/weight-loss claims.

**Result:** Pending.

### EXP-002 — TikTok latest-post distribution investigation + authentic-food creative pivot

**Status:** Running (investigation only; no new-content posting initiated)  
**Created:** 2026-10-12  
**Objective:** Establish why the new October TikTok Reel reported 2 views and test whether *authentic, distinct* content better engages users once distribution eligibility is confirmed.  
**Hypothesis:** Low views may reflect privacy/For You eligibility, reporting sync, or distribution/content mismatch. The currently available information does NOT distinguish these. Real kitchen footage or real app walk-through may generate stronger watch/qualified actions than a subtly animated static poster **if the platform distributes both normally**.  
**Source evidence:** `docs/INCIDENT_2026-10-12_NEW_REELS_LOW_DISTRIBUTION.md`; native Instagram latest Reel screenshot; Metricool exact latest TikTok canonical video (Oct 11).  
**Audience:** Saudi healthy-meal subscription prospects; follower geography is not evidence of buyer identity.  
**Control:** Existing newly posted 8-second TikTok video with 2 provider-observed views, 0 reported engagement (not an adequate control sample for causal claims).  
**Variant:** (A) Real preparation/packing Reel, then optionally (B) actual app-package explanation. Three fresh ideas are in the incident.  
**Primary diagnostic:** Native TikTok public/eligible distribution status + For You traffic and 24h/72h view/reach counts.  
**Secondary metrics:** Average watch seconds, shares/comments/profile taps; Instagram saves/profile visits when available.  
**Guardrail:** Never invent a kitchen clip/product; verify rights/menu/brand and commercial audio. No paid boosts or repost of identical media as a fake A/B test.  
**Start condition:** Obtain native TikTok Studio eligibility, account check and per-video visibility evidence; new approved authentic footage exists for any publishing experiment.  
**Stop/decision rule:** If a restriction is present, pursue documented appeal/permission fix. Otherwise test at least two meaningfully different eligible, public posts with comparable 24h/72h/7d measurements, without promising view thresholds or attributing organic distribution to a particular hook.  
**Result:** Latest TikTok remains at 2 Metricool views in latest Oct 12 checks; no verified technical cause or new creative test. Instagram last Reel: 500 views / 3 interactions / zero profile activity.  
**Learning:** Insufficient evidence to explain poor TikTok reach. New concepts must be original/real footage first; static poster is paused as an organic Reel default.  
**Decision after test:** Pending TikTok Studio evidence and subsequent experiments.
